<div class="academic-profile" markdown="1">

Hi! I am a final-year Ph.D. student in the Department of [Industrial Engineering and Operations Research](https://ieor.columbia.edu/) at Columbia University, advised by [Prof. Adam Elmachtoub](https://www.columbia.edu/~ae2516/).

<div class="research-focus" markdown="1">

**My research centers on socially responsible operations.** More specifically, I study how to design fair pricing systems for transportation and energy using optimization and machine learning.

</div>

I received my M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), where I was advised by [Prof. Il-Chul Moon](https://aai.kaist.ac.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=sub2_1&wr_id=3). I also hold B.S. degrees in Industrial and Systems Engineering and Electrical Engineering from KAIST.

_I am excited to be on the 2026–2027 academic job market._
